# Timely - AI Research Assistant for African SMEs

RAG system that allows AI to access and reason over internal datasets, research archives, and knowledge base 
Built with AI to solve a real business problem: fragmented follow-ups leading to lost contracts for SMEs in Lagos.


## Architecture (Maps to AI/ML Engineer JD)
- **RAG System:** Retrieves from fragmented business interactions, synthesizes into searchable intelligence
- **LLM:** Llama 3 on Manus - Prompt engineering (Sir tone: Professional/Warm/Casual), Structured output, Function calling
- **Agentic Workflow:** Multi-step automated research - AI independently retrieves data via SQL/API, analyzes, generates outputs, initiates actions (Tuesday 9am WAT scheduling)
- **Human-in-the-loop:** Verification where expert judgment needed
- **Optimization:** Latency, cost, accuracy tracking

## Validation
10 interviews Yaba/Ikeja Lagos, 2 real estate pilots Lagos, 4 hours faster reply reported

Built for Lagos SMEs where business data is fragmented across WhatsApp, voice notes, and memory - converts raw Lagos business data into searchable intelligence and decision-support, same as African Intelligence Engine goal.
